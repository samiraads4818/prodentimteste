About ProDentim

A product positioned around the oral microbiome.

ProDentim is marketed as an oral wellness supplement that brings together probiotic strains and other formula components. The concept is to add a probiotic-focused option to a person's everyday oral-care conversation.

The materials provided describe a blend containing approximately 3.5 billion probiotic strains. As with any supplement, individual questions about suitability are best discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
